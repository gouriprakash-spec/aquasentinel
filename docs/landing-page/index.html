Live readings
Each pull reads live data from the USGS Penn's Landing gauge (01467200) and the PHL weather station (or the Open-Meteo weather model when the station's rain record is incomplete), then estimates how safe the Center City tidal Delaware reach is for recreation right now.
How the status is decided: a disclosed rainfall rule, not a model. The reach is flagged Unsafe when rain over the two previous calendar days (a 48-hour window, midnight to midnight, the same window the rule was derived on) is at least the rule threshold mm. A model trained on past DRBC E. coli samples taken near shore at Penn's Landing runs alongside it; “rule/model agreement” shows how strongly that model agrees with the rule, and is not the model's own verdict. A nearby, fresh, actively or recently overflowing combined-sewer outfall can also force the reach Unsafe, overriding the rainfall rule — the banner then names the outfall instead of showing the rain amount. Ben Franklin Bridge and Navy Yard are DRBC channel stations, shown for context.
